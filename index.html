What even is PRISM anyway?

PRISM is an independent software initiative focused on building systems that prioritize people over short-term economic optimization and metric-driven design.


Alright, but what does it actually do?

PRISM develops long-term Projects designed for clarity, modularity, and user control, without artificial restrictions or engagement traps.

Its current primary Project is Project Noema, a modular game console platform for browsers. (You can access it here.)

Projects aren't tied to one thing either. They form a long-term collection of systems, each with its own purpose.

You could even think of them as a family.


That's cool. Is that all though?

No. Beyond Projects, PRISM also builds tools intended for developers outside of PRISM.
